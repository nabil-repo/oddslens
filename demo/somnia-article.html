In what marks a defining technical milestone for high-performance decentralized systems, the Somnia engineering team announced today that the Shannon Testnet has sustained execution benchmarks exceeding 100,000 TPS under intense parallel workload.

The breakthrough stems from Somnia’s innovative Multi-Stream consensus and custom EVM bytecode compiler, which eliminates single-threaded execution bottlenecks while retaining full compatibility with Solidity smart contracts.

DreamDEX Liquidity Surges on Fast Finality

The primary beneficiary of this throughput is DreamDEX, the native Central Limit Order Book protocol. While conventional decentralized exchanges are hindered by 12-second block intervals, DreamDEX matches orders in real-time.

Binary event contracts on Somnia ecosystem milestones have seen rapid trader adoption. Market participants are actively trading directional contracts on whether network TPS will hold above 100k during upcoming validator epochs.

"Consumer applications — whether inline browser lenses, social betting bots, or gaming settlement — require true real-time performance. Somnia delivers that speed on-chain," noted a core validator.
